About KIBOE FarmSync

Built for farmers,
by people who understand the farm

KIBOE FarmSync was created to help smallholder and commercial farmers take full control of their operations. We combine practical farm management with modern technology so every farmer can plan with confidence and grow with clarity.

Whether you grow maize in Trans Nzoia or raise dairy cows in Kiambu, FarmSync keeps everything organised, secure and one tap away.

  • Simple, mobile-friendly tools that work well on slow connections
  • Data stays private and is owned by you — always
  • Designed around Kenyan crops, seasons and farming realities
  • A growing community marketplace and knowledge centre
Farm management suite

Run your whole farm from one dashboard

Four focused modules that work together so nothing slips through the cracks.

Crops & Fields

Record each crop, its variety, planted area and expected harvest. See growing, harvested and failed crops side by side.

  • Planting & harvest tracking
  • Field area & variety records
  • Seasonal history

Livestock & Health

Manage herds of any size. Log breeds, births, vaccination schedules and milk output for every animal.

  • Individual animal records
  • Vaccination & health tracking
  • Milk production logs

Income & Expenses

Track every shilling in and out of the farm. Understand costs, revenue and true profitability.

  • Categorised transactions
  • Net profit calculation
  • Annual & monthly summaries

Reports & Supplies

Generate instant reports, monitor farm supplies and keep inventory levels healthy all season long.

  • One-click farm reports
  • Supply & inventory tracking
  • Low-stock alerts
